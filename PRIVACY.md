# Privacy Policy - SmartXML

**Last updated: March 28, 2026**

## Overview

SmartXML is a macOS utility that displays XML files from SAP S/4HANA extractions in a readable table format. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data Collection

**SmartXML does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- All XML files are processed locally on your Mac
- No data is sent to any server or third-party service
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry is used
- No user accounts or registration are required
- No cookies or local storage beyond standard macOS app preferences

## File Access

SmartXML only accesses files that you explicitly open via the file picker or drag-and-drop. The app runs in a sandboxed environment and cannot access files without your permission.

## Third-Party Services

SmartXML does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/stefenry/SmartXML/issues).
